01 The account

A fast-growing host. Then a fight over who owned the story.

↘

I am Shreyansh Rathore. I wrote this account as the founder and CEO identity shown in the supplied evidence. I describe starting TreeMC Host, building the website, database, Discord bot and panel, and growing the community to roughly 1,800 members with my business partner, Mayank “Manku”.

According to that account, Shrey temporarily stepped away for personal or practical reasons and handed Mayank the source code, the treemc.host domain, and Discord ownership. Shrey says they told Mayank they were not leaving TreeMC permanently and would keep standing with the hosting through regular Instagram posts and videos. During the period that followed, Abdullah and Ekam were reportedly given CEO roles. The page publisher describes what happened next as a betrayal and disputes the way the Founder title is now represented.

The dispute escalated after I say Mayank banned me. I allege that Mayank, Abdullah, and Ekam sell Minecraft servers and VPS products built on “carded VPS” or “hijacked VPS” resources, and that the public-facing business is not what it claims to be.
